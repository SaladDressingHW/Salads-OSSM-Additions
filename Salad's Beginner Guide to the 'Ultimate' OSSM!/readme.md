# Welcome!
Are you **sick and tired** of **underwhelming** machines that are just copy pastes of each other? **Annoying** stroke depth adjustment by changing fly wheel radius? The same sinusoidal back and forth movement with **no pattern control**? **Underwhelming force** that **stalls** at anything bigger than a **toothpick**?
Ohhh do I have a solution for you! :DD

All tongue in cheek aside, that solution is called the **OSSM**, pronounced "awesome" and short for "Open Source Sex Machine"! As the name implies, it is an open souirce sex machine project, hosted on the official OSSM Github [here](https://github.com/KinkyMakers/OSSM-hardware/tree/main), with it's development and community based on the KinkyMakers Discord (I know, Discord for developing, bleh) [here](https://discord.gg/NcueGt87u).
The main features that sets it apart from other machines is real time adjustable stroke length and depth, pattern control with a big selection as well as documentation how to make your own, compactness, and strength. And all this at a relatively low price. It is 3d printed, and relatively easy and beginner friendly to assemble with great documentation. All other parts are of the shelf components you can easily get at places like aliexpress or amazon. Pick your poison :) I recommend checking out the repository for more information on the project.

## Do I need an alternative design?

Now while the machine is already quite powerful as is, with a pushing force of 12kgf (meaning it can push 12kg), it may not prove to be enough force for many users that, like myself, like to use **BIG** toys. From my and many other users experience, the standard build of the OSSM tends to stall with larger toys. Where that point is, is different from person to person. For some, it happens at sizes like large bad dragon toys, for others that is already the case with medium sized toys, especially knotted ones. That stalling is even more so the case for people with strong pelvic floors, especially if they cramp a lot during stimulation. This reasoning applies to both vaginal and anal use, although anal generally requires more force than vaginal use.

If you want to test out how much force you need, a hacky but simple way of doing that is taking the biggest toy that you want to use with it, place it on a scale and sit down on it. Put on all the force you can and try to push the toy out a bit for good measure. Add a few kg of redundancy to the biggest value you see on the scale, and you have your required force.
If that value is below 12kg, you are probably good to go with the standard design! But I do recommend to keep reading, there are a two other things to consider as well!

<br>

The first thing to consider is toy weight! The standard design uses a MGN12H linear rail. It's definitely a very great and capable rail that works for most toys, but the longer the rail length and therefor possible stroke depth, the higher the load will be on the bearing, that being the carriage block. This goes along with weight of toys. So, if you use bigger toys, that are also heavier and require longer stroke length to use the whole length on the machine, you multiply the load on the rail mounting. The force gets so high, that it can damage the rail over time and/or make it very loud.
So, the logical conclusion, at least mine, is to throw a bigger, beefier rail at it!

Now what you essentially need to check is the weight of your toys! The rail length you will choose does matter here, but as a general rule of thumb I recommend skipping on the standard design if you want to use toys heavier than 1.5kg!

<br>

The second thing that may influence your decision, is your expected "progress". Do you expect to size up over time? How much do you expect to size up? Will that push you over the edge where you might need a stronger machine? This is worth thinking a bit about, or you might have to upgrade at some point, which means you essentially spend the same money twice.

## So what is the alternative?
For the above described personal gripes I have with the standard design, I developed my own machine. That machine is called the **Capstan OSSM XL**, or **Capstan XL**. It completely revamps the Hardware to enable use of a motor ~twice as powerful as the original one, giving you a total of 32kgf pushing force! By this point it has been tried and tested by many users, and the feedback incorporated into multiple updates and iterations, to this day.
