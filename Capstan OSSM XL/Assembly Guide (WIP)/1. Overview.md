# Welcome!
So you have decided on building a Capstan OSSM XL, or at least are thinking about it! It's always good to take a look before starting the process as well, so good on you! This doc is the first part of the Assembly Guide, and gives an Overview of the process. So let's get started!

<br>

## Step 1: Sourcing parts
This is probably the most unattractive part. This build has a lot of parts that you need. There are two ways that you can go about that!

#### The easy route:
 Head over to my [Etsy](https://www.etsy.com/shop/SaladsSexMachines) and order a Hardware Kit! Every item that you need to buy (except tools) is included there. Some items like the recommended rope can be hard to source in some parts of the world, so this might be a good option for you

#### The DIY route:
 Head over to the [BOM](https://github.com/SaladDressingHW/Salads-OSSM-Additions/tree/main/Capstan%20OSSM%20XL/Hardware) and source everything yourself! Quite a few things you will have to look for locally. More info in the following doc, [`Tools And Parts.md`](./2.%20Tools%20And%20Parts.md).

<br>

## Step 2: Print your parts
Again, you can do this two ways!

#### Printed Parts Kit:
3D printed part kits are ready to purchase, again from my [Etsy](https://www.etsy.com/shop/SaladsSexMachines). You can also message me to ask about personalizion options!

#### Print it yourself:
Head over to the [directory with the 3d printed parts](https://github.com/SaladDressingHW/Salads-OSSM-Additions/tree/main/Capstan%20OSSM%20XL/Hardware/3D%20printed%20parts/HG20). There are .stl and .3mf files both. The .3mf files include the settings and print orientations I use, and in some cases also painted supports. There are also .step files for all parts and in some cases .f3d as well so you can edit parts in case you want/need to modify something. More infos are provided in the following document, [`Tools And Parts.md`](./2.%20Tools%20And%20Parts.md). 
**Warning:** This project is **not** beginners friendly to print. You are expected to have some experience with 3D printing and a solid understanding of print settings and print optimization!

<br>

## Step 3: Prerequisites
There are still some other things that need to be done before assembly. Namely programming your Motor, flashing your PCB and glueing your Capstan Drum. Details and tutorials on how to do that are in the third document, [`Prerequisistes.md`](./3.%20Prerequisites.md).

<br>

## Step 4: Assembly
With everything sourced, printed and prepared, the machine can finally get assembled! This process is documented in the fourth and final document, [`Assembly.md`](./4.%20Assembly.md).

<br>
